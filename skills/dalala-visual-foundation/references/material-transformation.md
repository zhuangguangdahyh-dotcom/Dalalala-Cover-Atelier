# Material Transformation

## Separate anchors from freedoms

For each supplied asset, write two lists before composing.

**Identity or fact anchors** may include a person's face and expression, a product's geometry and material, a real location, a chart value, a legal or medical document, or an action that proves the story.

**Composition freedoms** may include background clutter, unused body area, framing, empty surroundings, repeated instances, depth layers, lighting emphasis, local colour, decorative texture, and any pixel that does not carry identity or evidence.

Preserve the first list. Actively redesign the second.

## Transformation levels

- **T0 factual lock:** crop edge and light correction only. Use for evidence that cannot be visually reinterpreted.
- **T1 spatial rearrangement:** cutout, reposition, scale, background cleanup or replacement.
- **T2 structural transformation:** change shot size, crop through the subject, enlarge a meaningful part, rotate, mask, occlude, extend, rebuild foreground or background, add perspective or directional motion.
- **T3 thematic reconstruction:** repeat or segment imagery, create extreme scale, place the subject inside type or negative shape, convert a body part or object into the visual metaphor, or reconstruct the apparent action and space while preserving anchors.

Lifestyle, pet, and non-evidence images normally permit at least T2. Use T3 when the topic depends on exaggeration, pressure, confusion, speed, choice, transformation, or another visible metaphor. Human IP and products also permit T2 around their protected anchors.

## Theme-to-action translation

Translate the decisive word into one visible action. Examples:

- pressure → compression, collision, reduced headroom
- confusion → broken route, repeated alternatives, conflicting directions
- growth → rising scale, expanding interval, upward path
- isolation → small subject against one continuous field
- comparison → aligned split with one controlled difference
- authority → monumental scale, stable baseline, restrained support detail
- intimacy → close crop, small distance, soft depth, shared frame

Do not represent a topic only with an icon or sticker when crop, scale, space, depth, repetition, or typography can carry it.

## Proof of transformation

Hide the title and decoration. The source treatment should still support the topic and selected spatial grammar. If the result is merely the original image on a new background, record why T1 is sufficient or rebuild it.
