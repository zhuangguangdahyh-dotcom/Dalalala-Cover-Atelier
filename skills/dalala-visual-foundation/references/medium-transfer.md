# Medium Transfer

Transfer relationships and information jobs from a reference; rebuild dimensions, density, and reading time for the destination.

## Cover, poster, and advertisement

Use one promise, one protagonist, and one fast reading path. A poster may tolerate longer viewing and richer atmosphere; a social cover must survive thumbnail scale. Advertising also needs a clear product or offer proof and a final action point.

## PPT

Assign each slide one job: open, state, explain, compare, prove, sequence, map, quote, or close. Use the corresponding template family. Across a deck, repeat alignment, margins, type scale ratios, colour roles, and module rhythm while varying page grammar to match each slide's content.

## Resume

Treat identity and target role as the protagonist. Experience needs chronological or achievement hierarchy; skills need grouped evidence; numbers need controlled emphasis. Editorial columns, margin notes, timelines, modular evidence, and one restrained identity field transfer well. Decorative complexity must never slow scanning.

## Long graphic and report

Use editorial narrative plus sequential flow. Alternate dense evidence with breathing pages or fields. Repetition creates trust; planned exceptions signal chapter changes and key conclusions.

## Aspect-ratio transfer

Rebuild these five items for every ratio: protagonist position and crop, title silhouette, dominant empty shape, reading path, and secondary-content capacity. Keep the template's locked relationships, not its original coordinates.

## Colour independence

Reference colours reveal contrast roles, not mandatory swatches. Re-select colours from the current subject, emotion, brand, medium, lighting, and accessibility needs. Preserve role relations such as dominant, support, and accent; warm and cool separation; or light and dark hierarchy. Never say a layout belongs to a fixed palette.
